A top row, and panels that fold into strips

Approved design, 2026-11-11. One row along the window's top holds the window buttons, then Projects, Todo and Manager in the panels' order, then the project's name and actions. Every panel and strip starts below it. A row button turns its panel on or off; the « or » in a panel's header folds it into a labelled strip in its own place, and the strip opens it again. Cards and terminals are made up; cyan rings mark what each step is about.
